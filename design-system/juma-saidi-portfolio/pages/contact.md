# Contact page

Inherit the master theme and the shared Home/About typography, container, navigation, footer, and reduced-motion behavior.

- Hero: small mono label, two-line Geist headline, 36–72px responsive type, restrained blue emphasis, readable Inter introduction.
- Contact section: desktop editorial split at 1024px, stacked on mobile. Direct email is a single bordered row; only verified social links appear. No repeated contact cards or availability claims.
- Form: one 16px rounded frame, secondary surface at low opacity, 16px inputs, visible labels, clear focus and validation states. Four visible fields only. Delivery unavailability is explicit, with a working email fallback.
- Closing sections: subtle secondary background for professional openness; concise personal statement and one work link. Omit FAQ to keep focus on contacting an individual.
- Interactions: shared gentle Motion reveals; minor link and arrow transitions; no continuous decorative effects. Pending spinner respects reduced motion.
- Accessibility: semantic sections, fieldset/legend, linked error text, first-error focus, announced feedback, keyboard controls, no horizontal overflow at 320px.
- Content: no invented profiles, response promises, prices, location commitments, or claims of current hiring/freelance availability.
